# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Student-led organisations, club officers, and project leads evaluating Club as the operating system for their org. They arrive on the marketing site to understand what the product does, whether it feels credible, and whether it is worth signing up for.

## Product Purpose

Club is a multi-tenant SaaS platform for student organisations. The marketing site should communicate that Club gives each org an isolated workspace with tasks, members, teams, plugins, and role-based permissions. Success means visitors quickly understand the product, trust that it is real and built for their workflow, and move toward sign-up.

## Brand Personality

Premium techie, precise, credible.

The voice should feel product-led and technically informed, not generic or hype-driven. It should sound like a real system with clear boundaries, not a startup pitch deck. Copy should explain the product with confidence and specificity.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS landing pages with vague promises, stock buzzwords, and feature soup
- AI-slop marketing copy that says everything and explains nothing
- Mascot-driven or overly playful startup branding
- Corporate, institutional, or bureaucratic school-admin visuals

## Design Principles

- Show the actual product rather than abstract claims
- Use specific product language and concrete workflow benefits
- Keep the visual system premium, technical, and restrained
- Make trust and credibility immediate through structure, hierarchy, and proof
- Avoid filler; every section should earn its place

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA.

Use strong contrast, visible focus states, and reduced-motion-friendly interactions. Marketing copy should remain understandable on its own without relying on motion, visual gimmicks, or decorative treatment.
